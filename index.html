Presentation Mode off

Hide Nexora and take it off the taskbar while I present
For your own presentation workflow. Turning it on hides the window and removes it from the taskbar and alt-tab; turning it off puts it back at exactly the position and size it had. Nexora keeps running the whole time — your conversation, your screenshot and everything else are still there, and the tray icon still works. Push-to-talk and screenshot keep working too, and while the mode is on they no longer pull the window forward, so a capture is simply waiting for you when you come back. It is a local switch and nothing more: it does not touch Teams, Zoom, Meet, Slack or your browser, and it makes no attempt to change what screen-capture software can record.

Screen Share Privacy OFF

Hide Nexora from screen sharing
Nexora stays on your own monitor but is left out of screen shares and recordings that go through supported Windows capture. This is not a guarantee: a phone pointed at the screen, some remote-desktop tools, and capture paths Windows does not cover will still see it. Test it before you rely on it.
Also hide the taskbar button and alt-tab entry
The switch above keeps Nexora out of captures but not out of your taskbar, where the button stays visible to anyone looking at your screen. This removes the button and the alt-tab entry with it. The tray icon and the hotkeys are then how you get the window back — learn one before you turn this on.
